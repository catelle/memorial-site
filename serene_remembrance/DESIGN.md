## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an enduring, reverent digital sanctuary for memorials, tributes, and celebrations of life. The visual language blends classic editorial prestige with understated, contemporary tranquility. The design must evoke deep respect, gentle solace, and timeless dignity—avoiding both clinical coldness and heavy, somber gloom.

The design movement centers on **Refined Editorial Warmth with Tactile Serenity**:
- Generous breathing room and contemplative negative space to let memories and prose resonate.
- Tactile, stationery-inspired textures through parchment-inspired tints, delicate metallic accents, and nuanced surfaces.
- Subtle, whisper-quiet micro-interactions that feel measured, deliberate, and respectful rather than flashy or urgent.
- Restraint as the primary aesthetic principle: structural lines mimic hairline deckle-edge foil, while layouts treat imagery and words with curatorial care.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the interface in warmth, solace, and graceful longevity:

- **Primary (`#C5A880` - Champagne Gold)**: Represents honoring, timeless warmth, and gentle illumination. Used intentionally for focal moments, elegant rule dividers, active states, key tribute actions, and delicate hairline accents.
- **Secondary (`#2C3038` - Rich Charcoal Slate)**: Provides quiet, authoritative contrast for primary typography and foundational structures without the harsh finality of pure black.
- **Tertiary (`#8A94A6` - Muted Heather Slate)**: Delivers soft, contemplative legibility for supporting metadata, timestamps, location markers, and subtle iconography.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Ivory / Alabaster)**: The quiet canvas resembling heavy cotton archival paper. Secondary tonal surfaces lean into warm cream (`#F4EFEA`) and warm veil-white (`#FFFFFF`) for cards and elevated media vessels.

Semantic applications:
- **Surface Foundations**: Base canvas is `#FAF8F5`; elevated layers transition into pure `#FFFFFF` with whisper-fine `#EBE4DC` and `#C5A880` (at 20% opacity) border contours.
- **Text & Ink**: Headers and commemorative titles rely on `#2C3038`; long-form biography copy uses `#3D424D` for optimal optical softness; supporting dates and locations rest in `#697386`.

## Typography

The typography pairs the regal, emotional cadence of **Playfair Display** with the crisp, human, and highly legible geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs for Emotion**: Headline and display sizes use Playfair Display to honor names, life dates, quotes, and eulogies. It delivers an archival, literary presence reminiscent of bespoke memorial stationery.
- **Modern Sans for Utility**: Body and metadata levels use Plus Jakarta Sans to render biographies, itineraries, service details, addresses, and tribute messages with crystalline legibility across all screen densities.
- **Rhythmic Kerning and Leading**: Extended line heights throughout long-form body copy prevent cognitive fatigue during emotional reading sessions. Small labels and metadata tokens use slight uppercase letter-spacing to mirror fine engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a **Centred, Column-Constrained Editorial Grid** framed by generous margins that invoke high-grade paper prints.

- **Desktop Framework**: A 12-column layout maxing out at `1160px` container width for biographical reading and media galleries, with section margins scaling to `margin` (`3rem`). Long-form tribute stories stay constrained to a centered 8-column spine (`720px` max-width) to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet Adaptation**: At 768px–1024px, the layout reduces to 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters, preserving breathing room around tribute portraits and memorial timelines.
- **Mobile Harmony**: Under 768px, single-column linear flow takes precedence. The canvas collapses to `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) with `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`) to keep imagery intimate and text readable without horizontal tension.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing relies on deliberate increments (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between content milestones (e.g., life chapters, service locations, floral condolences) to invite natural pauses.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic neon glows, and abrupt 3D extrusions. Visual depth is established through **archival paper stacking, luminous ambient glows, and hairline borders**:

1. **Flat Tonal Base (`Ground`)**: Pure `#FAF8F5` representing the archival backdrop.
2. **Elevated Keepsake Layer (`Surface Level 1`)**: Used for condolence cards, service programs, and photo frames. White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px hairline border of `#EBE4DC` accompanied by a soft, diffused ambient veil: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 48, 56, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating Sanctuary (`Surface Level 2`)**: Used for focused tribute modals, condolence submission drawers, and pinned audio eulogies. Background `#FFFFFF` framed with a dual border: an inner 1px `#FFFFFF` highlight and an outer 1px gold hairline (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)`), anchored by an ambient drop shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(44, 48, 56, 0.08)`.
4. **Delicate Gold Hairlines**: Structural borders consistently use `1px solid` warm champagne tones (`#E6DDD2` resting, `#C5A880` focused or highlighted), emulating gold leaf on bespoke paper goods.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, reflecting crisp, classic paper stationary with gently eased corners:

- Standard elements (interactive cards, input fields, media frames) feature subtle `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii, imparting timeless precision while preventing harsh sharp edges.
- Larger storytelling containers and memorial service banners utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to gracefully cradle portraits and tribute groups.
- Avatars, floral tribute pins, and candle-lighting toggles retain full circular form (`50%` / round) as symbolic circles of life and continuous remembrance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Honor / Light a Candle")**: Background in rich champagne gold (`#C5A880`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`) or rich charcoal (`#2C3038`), finished with soft corners (`0.25rem`), a gentle hover shift to `#B5966B`, and a luminous golden halo on focus.
- **Secondary / Ghost ("Share Memory", "Service Program")**: Transparent background, 1px champagne border (`#C5A880`), text in `#2C3038`. Hover state introduces a warm alabaster tint (`#F4EFEA`) with transitions timed at `250ms ease-out`.

### Tribute & Memory Cards
- Ivory or white paper background framed by a hairline border (`#EBE4DC`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Subtle quotation ornament or italicized Playfair Display excerpt leading into Plus Jakarta Sans tribute content.
- Pinned bottom bar displaying sender relationship, city/country, and timestamp in `#8A94A6`.

### Form Fields & Condolence Inputs
- Background in subtle warm alabaster (`#FBF9F6`) with a 1px border in `#DFD7CC`.
- Labels styled in uppercase `label-sm` with letter-spacing.
- Focus state elevates the field with a `#FFFFFF` fill and a `#C5A880` hairline rim without harsh high-contrast rings.

### Memorial Timeline & Schedule
- A delicate vertical champagne line (`1px` width, `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)`) anchoring service moments (Viewing, Celebration, Reception).
- Time milestones set in Playfair Display serif, accompanied by address links and streaming badges formatted in compact, pill-accented ivory tags.

### Media & Portrait Vessels
- Portrait photographs framed with an inset warm border and a generous external ivory matting mimicking a framed keepsake.
- Subtle gold badge counter for condolences, shared photos, and virtual candles.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Discreet `0.25rem` rounded boxes with a 1px `#C5A880` frame. Checked state displays a soft champagne fill with an ivory tick.
