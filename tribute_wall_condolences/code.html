“She taught us that genuine intellect without compassion is empty vanity.”

In 1994, as a bewildered first-year doctoral candidate at St. Hugh's, I nearly packed my trunks to return home. Professor Eleanor noticed my despair before I could utter a word. She invited me into her study, handed me a pen, and spent three hours revising my thesis hypothesis line by line. Over the ensuing thirty years, her mentorship guided not only my research career, but my moral compass as a father and educator. The world owes her a debt of quiet brilliance.
